[handwritten: cost / 3-6b / october / 1712]



T H E 
CIRCE
O F
Signior Giovanni Battista Gelli 
Of the Academy of
FLORENCE. 

Consisting of Ten DIALOGUES
between Men transform'd into Beasts:
Giving a lively Representation of the
various Passions, and many Infelicities 
of Humane Life. 

---

Done out of *Italian*,
By Mr. *THO. BROWN.*

---

———*pauci dignoscere possunt*
*Vera bona, atq; illis multum diversa, remotâ*
*Erroris nebulâ, quid enim ratione timemus*
*Aut cupimus*——— Juv. Sat. 10.

---

LONDON:
Printed for *John Nutt* near *Stationers-Hall.* 1702.
