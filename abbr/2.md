---
title: "2. The Oyster and the Mole"
---

Well, my dear Ulysses, and what do your countrymen say to you? Are any of them willing to become men again? 

Not one, it's true. But I've only discoursed with those two whom you've showed me, one of whom it seems had been a fisherman in days of yore, the other a plowman.
