*Ulysses.* I do, for 'tis certainly true.

*Dog.* You must consequently grant then that we are wiser than you, because we perform our Operations far more wisely than you do yours. And to convince you of this Truth, let me only desire you to consult your own Experience. Consider diligently the Operations of each of our Kinds, beginning at the lowest Class of Beasts, and you will find the Ant to be so provident and wise, that he lays up Store enough in the Summer to subsist him the Winter following; observe with what Artifice and Cunning the Spiders lay their Snares to entrap certain little Insects on which they feed; and how cautiously your Wasps and many other of the like nature, hide themselves in Holes under Ground at those Seasons, which, if they ventured abroad, would be too severe and pinching for them.
