*Ulysses.* A true Lawyer, let me tell you, never values that, so long as he has the fingering of their Money. But I remember a merry dispute happened once in one of our Universities in *Greece* about the point of Precedence, that is to say, who ought to go first, the Lawyers, or the Physicians, and after a long canvassing and arguing of the matter, it was at last decided in favour of the Lawyers, and the reason that was given for't was this, that at all your public Executions the Thief always goes first, and the Hangman follows after.

*Hare.* Well, when I perceived what little content their was to be had in any of these Conditions, thought I to my self, if there's such a thing as tranquillity to be found on this side Heaven, it must certainly be among the Priests, who secluding themselves from the noise and hurry of the World, enjoy a peaceful and learned Retirement in their Cells, possessing all things in common, and suffering themselves to be governed by one of their own
