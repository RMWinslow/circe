Nature does not Voluntarily provide for? Whereas that noble Animal called Man, Lord of all the Universe, as he fondly thinks himself, must Manure the Earth, and Plough it, and Sow it, and Harrow it, if he would have it furnish him with one single Meal, as it does *gratis* all the Year round to the rest of the Creation.

*Ulysses.* He may even thank himself for this, since nothing but nice Eating will please him, whereas would he feed upon the Fruits, which the Earth produces of its own accord, as the Beasts do, this doughty Argument would fall down to the ground.

*Mole.* But pray Sir, what Herbs, what Seeds, what Fruits does this Mother Earth produce of her own accord, unless she is assisted by Art, that are sufficient for human sustenance.

*Ulysses.* Why have you not read that the good People of the *Golden-Age* had no other nourishment than what the Ground spontaneously afforded?

*Mole.* Alas Friend, and dost thou believe these idle Stories, thou that seemest to be so Wise a Man?
