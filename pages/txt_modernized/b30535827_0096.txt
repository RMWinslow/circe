Well, nothing supported me under these troubles, but the comfortable reflection, that when this cursed Law-Tempest was over, I should ride safe in Harbour, free from all Storms and Hurricanes of this Nature; but it seems I reckoned without my Host, for the quite contrary happened. To make short of my Story, after a cursed expensive Suit I got my share of the Estate, part of which lay in Land and Houses, and part in ready Money; and now my Vexations came double and treble upon me. Having been always used to be governed, I found my self at a damned loss when I came to be my own Master, and behaved my self as awkwardly in my new Sovereignty, as a Country-Bumkin in a new Suit of Clothes. As little as I knew of the World, I had so much Experience of it, that I did not think it safe to trust others, however there was no avoiding it.
