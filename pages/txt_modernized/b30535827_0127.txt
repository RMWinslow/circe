*Ulysses.* There are but few such timorous Coxcombs as these.

*Goat.* Those that are not affected with these things have others that discompose them; some are always so much under the fear and apprehension of Sickness, that they cannot ever be said to be really well, denying themselves those things for prevention which Nature hath designed for an innocent Pleasure and Refreshment, abridging themselves of those Necessaries which a regular Appetite might modestly require. These Persons make themselves Slaves to Rules, Customs and Habits, so that if the Season chance to vary from its usual Course, they are presently under so great a Consternation, that it very often throws them into a fit of Sickness.

*Ulysses.* Such pitiful Hen-hearted Wretches as these don't deserve the name of Men.
