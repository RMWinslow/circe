Besides this Fantastic variety of Tastes provokes you to drink more than Nature can well carry off; By which means you come to be visited by Catarrhs, Defluxions, Rheumatisms, Tumours, Gouts, Palsies, and a Thousand such Distempers, which never happen to any of us.

*Ulysses.* I must confess that what thou sayest is partly true.

*Snake.* In the next place, consider, that Nature has a greater kindness for us, she has not subjected us to the Tyranny of any irregular Appetites, so that as we desire nothing but what is proper, and convenient for us, so we never exceed the bounds of Temperance, and what falls out very happy for us, we have not the Art of varying and mingling our meats, and consequently lie under no temptation of committing any excess.
