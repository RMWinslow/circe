But if it examins this Representation or *Idea* afresh, and begins to divest him of all those particular Circumstances which only belonged to that individual Man, and consider nothing but the human Nature abstractedly in him, it forms to itself an intellectual Operation, the result of which is this universal Knowledge, that human Nature is a corporeal Substance, mortal, and capable of perceiving Reason: And in this all Men equally agree.

*Elephant.* And what Perfection in itself has this universal Knowledge of your Intellect, than the particular Knowledge of our Senses?

*Ulysses.* Why a greater certainty, and is not that enough? In short, you know that a thing is so and not otherwise, and that you cannot be deceived, which the sensitive Knowledge can never pretend to give.

*Elephant.* And how does that appear?

*Ulysses.*
