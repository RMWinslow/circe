Then finding that I could not live like a Gentleman without a multitude of Servants, who are all of them Thieves and Pick-Pockets in their Nature, and busy their thoughts continually to abuse and cheat their Masters, I was resolved to drop my Gentility, and take Sanctuary in some other Station. At last after mature deliberation, I concluded that it would be the wisest course for me to place my self in some honourable Post at Court, and to bring this about, Employed all my own Interest and that of my Friends; for thought I, where can Happiness dwell so properly, as in the Magnificence and Ease of a Palace, near the Person of a Prince, who can command every thing? But good Heavens! how lamentably was I mistaken in my Politics?
