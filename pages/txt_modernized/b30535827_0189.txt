*Ulysses.* Right, but they are very few in comparison of those that hunt after Wealth, and pursue bodily Pleasure. And even of these few, Madam, the greatest part seek Virtue for no other end, but the Temporal advantage they propose to themselves by it, as to live Easier and Finer, to obtain Honour, to get Estates and the like. But such designing Gentlemen ought by means to be called virtuous, for they don't embrace Virtue for Virtue's sake, and because she's good, but to make their Markets of her. The first and most active desire of our Soul is to know the truth, and the Causes of things, in order to satisfy its Curiosity, which is the end of it, and not to propose to our selves any sordid lucre and interest by it, as those do, who knowing nothing but the Body, think on nothing but the Body and its pleasures; to which stupidity is owing all the miseries and infelicities of human life.
