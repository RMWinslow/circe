Not to tell you that Nature has ordered it thus on purpose, to give Man an opportunity to show his Ingenuity and Cunning, and how much superior he is to the Beasts.

*Mole.* For my part I should take it otherwise, as that he should never take any repose, or pass one easy comfortable Hour.
