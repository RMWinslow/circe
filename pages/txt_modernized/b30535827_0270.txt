*Elephant.* I was a *Grecian*, Born in the most famous City of *Athens*, where I applied my self a long time to the study of Philosophy, and my name was *Aglaophemus*. And now I have answered you your Question, pray tell me why you asked it. You know we Philosophers are an inquisitive sort of People, who make it our business to enquire after the causes of things, on purpose to gratify that itch of Knowledge, which every Man naturally has.

*Ulysses.* Heaven be praised, I have at last found out a lover of Truth, and one that may truly and properly call himself a Man. Know then, *Aglaophemus*, that *Circe* out of kindness to me has been pleased to give me Power to restore to their Shapes all the *Grecians* in this Island, whom she has transformed into Beasts, and to carry them home with me to their native Country, but upon this condition that they were willing to accept it.
