*Ulysses.* Perhaps I might, if they all acted after the same manner.

*Bullock.* 'Tis sufficient that you see the generality of them, and those that are most conspicuous for Quality, Wealth, Preferments, and the like, by whom the lesser part is always to be determined, to act villainously and unjustly. Now tell me I beseech you, if there were Justice naturally among you, as there is among us, or if you lived up to that Law which Nature has written in your Hearts, what occasion wou'd there be for you so many Laws, Statutes, Edicts and Decrees? Tho 'tis a common saying among you, I know, that Laws are like Cobwebs, which your Hornets and the like bulky Insects break with ease, while the poor Flies are entangled in them.
