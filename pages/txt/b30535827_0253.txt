*Bullock.* As much as is requisite for our State and Nature, and if you examin our actions, you'll soon find it to be so, and particularly when one of us fights with another, where you may see all of us rejoyce and congratulate the Conqueror, but those that are unprofitable are continually despised by us.

*Ulysses.* Whether this be true or no, you best know yourselves, neither will I contend with you about it. But what will you say to me of commutative Justice is that to be found among you?

*Bullock.* We have just as much of it as you have, that is to say, nothing at all on't, but with this remarkable difference, that we don't stand in need of it, since we possess every thing in common; whereas 'tis in a manner impossible for you to live without it, since those two everlasting Incendiaries call'd *Meum* and *Tuum* have been introduced into the World.
