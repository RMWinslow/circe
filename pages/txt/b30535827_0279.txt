*Elephant.* And what are they?

*Ulysses.* In the first place that he stands still, and secondly, that he is so small in bigness: For he moves with that incredible rapidity, that neither a Dart out of an Engine, nor an Arrow out of a Bow, bear any manner of comparison to him, since drawn by the *Primum Mobile*, he once a day goes round the Earth, which is so far distant from him. For which reason he must describe a much greater Circumference than that of the Terrestrial Globe, which they say is above Twenty two Thousand Miles in compass. Besides this, he is a hundred and sixty times bigger than the Earth, as you would plainly know if you were conversant in Mathematical Matters, which are no less certain to our understanding, than that the leaves of yonder Bay-tree are green, is so to your sight, in which particular I confess you are not mistaken, although you have not so much certainty of it as you would have, if you had the intellective faculty.

*Elephant.* And why so?
