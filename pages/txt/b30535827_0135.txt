[— the Goat. — 121]

*Ulysses.* True Friendship proceeds from the consideration of what is good and virtuous, of which you can't have the least Notion or Idea. Therefore that Friendship which is contracted amongst wicked Persons for some evil and base End, or sordid Gain and Advantage, may be more properly called a Combination, Plot or Conspiracy, than true Friendship. Now yours is nothing but meer Instinct and natural Inclinations; Friendship also should be voluntary and elected, which is beyond your Power to do.

*Goat.* If we are not susceptible of true Friendship, yet we are free from Fawning, Flattery, whose specious Pretences often do as much harm, as real Friendship can do good.

*Ulysses.* But by the Assistance of Reason and Experience in Conversation, we are capable of distinguishing the one from the other.

*Goat.* For my part, I cannot see how, since an expert Flatterer, can so well counterfeit the part of a true Friend, besides the Nature of Man is very prone to be pleased with Flattery, and you may be easily imposed upon in this particular.

*Ulysses.*
