*Ulysses.* The conclusion, I own, is undeniable, but the difficulty will be to prove it.

*Bullock.* Why if the premises are true, the conclusion must of course be so.

*Ulysses.* And do you then understand so much Logick.

*Bullock.* What wonder is it, since I was bred and born in *Greece*, where you know we are taught it from our Infancy.

*Ulysses.* Well, go on with your show.

*Bullock.* The first of these Propositions, I mean, that where there is no Justice there is no Virtue at all. I need not give my self the trouble to prove, since you have own'd that she comprehends all the Virtues, for which reason some have call'd her, as you observe, the system of all Virtue.

*Ulysses.* I own your Major, now go on with your Minor.
