But what means this Dog, fawning and wagging his Tail, and now he is come up to me, stands still? 'Tis the Nature of this Creature, I know, to be very fond of Man, and I suppose he's the gladder to see me, because those of my kind are so seldom seen in this Island. —— I believe, nay, I know it to be true by Experience, that Nature gave us those Senses that are not necessary for the Conservation of Life, only for our better being, and to make our Knowledge more perfect: Hence it comes to pass, that having their objects upon the Earth, they incline and depress our better part towards it, which, if they did not hinder it, would of its own Nature continually aspire to Heaven —— Bless me! how this Dog stares and looks at me?
