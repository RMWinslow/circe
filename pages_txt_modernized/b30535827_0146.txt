[132 — Ulysses, Circe, — Dial. V.]

*Ulysses.* You are a wonderful Philosopher.

*Hind.* My Husband was an excellent Philosopher, and I could not choose but improve by his Conversation; but besides, this sort of Philosophy comes to us naturally.

*Ulysses.* And yet all your Philosophy is not able to remedy one Defect so universally predominant in your Sex.

*Hind.* What is that?

*Ulysses.* To hear your own Tongue run so, that you are more desirous of talking, than to be Woman again, thanking the Gods so devoutly for that Favour, which intimates how very fond you are of it.

*Hind.* I am much in the right of it, in regard you use our Sex so barbarously, making mere Slaves of us, whereas we were originally designed for your Assistants and Associates, perverting the Order of Nature, for which you give no other Reason than that it is your Will and Pleasure.
