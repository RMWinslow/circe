And from whence does this proceed, but from our invincible and mighty Courage? which plainly appears when you take us Captives, for we bear Hunger and Thirst with incredible patience; nay, many of us voluntarily pine away and die, rather than remain with you, and prefer Death to a nasty Servitude. For this Reason when you have a mind to make any of us tame, you are forced to take our young ones, who not knowing what they do, as having never tasted the Life of Nature, suffer you to feed them familiarly by your enticing Tricks; by which means, they lose at once with their Liberty, that Vigour of Mind, and Strength of Body, that belongs to their Species. But to convince you beyond all possibility of a Reply that Nature has given us a greater share of Courage than you, she has made us less impatient of Pain and other Inconveniences, and this she has done, not only to the Males, but to the Females, who are no less apt than the Males to defend both themselves and their young
