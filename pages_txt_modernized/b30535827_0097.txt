And yet I found that neither of them were content with their Condition, but eternally Lamented and Complained: One, of the excessive Rains and Droughts that spoiled the Fruits of the Earth, the cruelty of Landlords, as likewise of the little esteem the World had of the poor Countrymen; the other of contrary Winds, unlucky accidents and dangers at Sea, Insurances, Shipwrecks, Pirates, and the perpetual Wars between Princes, that destroyed Navigation and Commerce.

*Ulysses.* Why every Body must expect to meet some vexation in this World. Even you, with all your boasted happiness, have something to trouble you.

*Hare.* Ay, but to our comfort be it said, where we have one, you have at least a thousand Crosses. But to go on with my History: during this time, partly to supply my self with the conveniences and necessaries of life, and partly to secure what was my own, from being taken out of my Hands
