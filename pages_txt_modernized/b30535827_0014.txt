And that Ulysses might know their Sentiments upon this Matter, she granted them the Power to speak and converse with him, just as they would have done when they were Men. Ulysses visits the whole Island, and discourseth with several of them, who gave him their Reasons why they rather choose to continue in that State, than to become Men again. At last he meets with one, who considering the Excellence of Man, and how much Superior he is to all other Creatures, by the means of his Reason and Understanding, is desirous to be restored to his former Shape. Having effected this by the intercession of Ulysses, first of all, as in Duty bound, he returns his Thanks to the All-wise, and Almighty Author of Nature; and thus they returned joyfully together to their own Country.
