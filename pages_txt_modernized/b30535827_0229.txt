'Tis indeed true, that those Animals that possess this Power of the Imagination in a more perfect degree, by which they have a clearer and more distinct Knowledge of things, seem to have a Memory: in which Class those of your Species without any Compliment to you, hold the principal Rank: for to appearance, you remember things better, and know them more distinctly, but particularly your Masters, than any Creature whatever. As for those other Kinds that possess it more imperfectly, they seem to remember less, as your flies, that when they are driven from any place, forget it immediately, and return to it again.
