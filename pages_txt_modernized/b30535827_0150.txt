*Ulysses.* You can manage these things well enough if you will.

*Hind.* And so we should things of greater moment if you did but permit us.

*Ulysses.* You had best leave off whilst you are well; for I remember a Story of a *Shoemaker*, who being commended for finding fault with a Buckle that was amiss in the Shoe of a Statue; thereupon grew so vain, as to run beyond his Last, and make some other very ridiculous Remarks.

*Hind.* I wish we could please you in any thing, but all we can do, can scarce deserve a good word from you.

*Ulysses.* You do us abundance of wrong; we respect and honour you above our selves.
