*Ulysses.* Rather if you please call it a natural inclination, and you will speak properly. Lastly to proceed to that Duty, which we are obliged to render to such, as we have received Benefits from, called by us thankfulness or gratitude, you will not pretend, I suppose, to have any thing like that among you.

*Bullock.* Why, have you not observed that many of us, not only relieve and assist one another, but are serviceable to Man, for the Food, or some other necessaries we receive from him. How often have Dogs generously saved the lives of their Masters, when they have been attacked by Assassins, or Robbers? How joyful and proud is the Horse to carry his owner. Nay not only domestick Animals, but the fiercest, the most savage Creatures have been known to express their gratitude in a most wonderful manner to their Benefactors and Deliverers.
