Covetousness also is a Vice to which we are utterly Strangers, because we have no distinction of *Meum* and *Tuum*, but possess all in common. The same may be said of many other Vices, that render your Lives unhappy; but never infected our Tribes: upon which Consideration, some of our wise *Grecians* have affirmed, that if Man is the chief of other Creatures, 'tis only in respect of his Miseries and Troubles.

*Ulysses.* Well, suppose we have many Evils that you have not, yet for our Comfort be it observed, we have likewise many good things that are not to be found among you.

*Lion.* And what are those in the name of Wonder?

*Ulysses.* Virtues.

*Lion.* So far from that, *Ulysses*, that I will farther maintain, that you don't possess one single Virtue, which is not greater, and more perfect in us.

*Ulysses.* I should be glad to hear you make out that Paradox to me.

Lion.
