[— and the Lion. — 149]

Love, that I will depend on her Word, and accost them. Gentle and Noble *Lions*, may Nature always furnish you with all things that conduce to your Ease and Pleasure in your present State, or Change you into one more acceptable to you; pray, be so kind to tell me, if there be any one amongst you that was a *Grecian*; for if it be agreeable to his Desires and Wish, he may this Day, by the mighty Power of Love, be restored to his pristine Shape, and return into his own Country.

*Lion.* I was a *Grecian*, as I perceive you are, if you speak your Mother Tongue.

*Ulysses.* I am a *Grecian*, my Name is *Ulysses*, if you ever heard of me.

*Lion.* Very often both in *Greece* and elsewhere in my Travels; but have you left off following the Wars, by which you acquired so much Fame and Honour, not only in *Greece*, but throughout the whole World also? or are you unfortunately driven upon these Coasts as I was?

*Ulysses.* It was not Fortune, but the desire of seeing the World, brought me hither; for *Greece* having conquered all her Enemies, and I having no way now left to acquire more Honour, I went Sea.

Lion.
