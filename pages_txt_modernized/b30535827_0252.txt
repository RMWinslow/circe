Cast your eyes upon all our Cities here in *Greece*, whether the administration of them is lodged in one single hand, or in a few, or in the People, and you will soon be convinced what authority and influence wicked Men have in all their public Assemblies, and how little the virtuous are regarded by them. Now this is wholly owing to the blindness, corruption, and partiality of those Persons, whose business it is to distribute rewards and punishments, who frequently suffer themselves to be so much influenced by private interest, envy, or some other irregular passion, that for the same gallant Action we see one Man preferred, and no notice taken of another, nay what is more monstrous, for the very same crime in all circumstances, one Man has been advanced to a Gallows, and another to a good Post in the Government.
