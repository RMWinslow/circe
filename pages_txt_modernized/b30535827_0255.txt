'Tis a plain case that you despise Virtue by the worthy Proverbs you daily Preach to your Children. Get money, my Son, honestly if thou canst, however get money. Virtue won't keep the Wolf from the Door, she's so sheepish-hearted. Money will make the Pot boil though the Devil has pist in the Fire. A Man without money is as a Man dead, take money from a Man, and cut off his Head, money is the best Picklock, and surest Friend; with a Hundred other notable sayings to the same purpose.
