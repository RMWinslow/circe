*Lion.* Before you go any farther; For what Reason did you Princes of *Greece* make War upon *Troy*?

*Ulysses.* To vindicate an injured Prince's Honour, whose Queen had been villainously stolen away by a treacherous lustful *Barbarian*.

*Lion.* You mean, to help a poor abject whining Cuckold to his Wife again, that had been a Strumpet before he ever married her, and did not deserve the meanest Scoundrel upon Earth to fall in her Quarrel.

*Ulysses.* Nay, you may ridicule the most serious things, if you please.

*Lion.* And pray, what principle of Virtue did those Gentlemen Rascals in red go upon those Inhabitants of Hollow Trees, and Hay Cocks, those Valiant Plunderers of Henroosts, those Heroic Scourers of Hedges, the Rubbish of High-ways and Bridges, the Refuse of Gaols and Gibbets, I mean your half-starved Wretches that ventured all for two pence a Day, by the same Token it was paid them in good substantial Drubs upon their Backs.
