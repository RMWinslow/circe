DIALOGUES.

      who had before his Transformation
      been a *Fisher-man*; and the *Mole*, who
      had been a *Plow-man*.

      who had been a *Physician*.

      Gentleman.

V.    ——the *Hind*, a *Grecian* Woman.
VI.   ——the *Lion*, a Sailor.

VIII. ——the *Dog*, a Gentleman of Learning.
