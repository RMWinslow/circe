*Ulysses.* It is impossible that you should be ignorant of Time. There are a great many of you that change Countries every Spring and Fall, as Swallows and Thrushes; and others hide themselves under the Earth, as Snakes, Badgers, &c.

*Goat.* This doth not in the least proceed from our knowledge of Time, but only from feeling the different Effects of it, for we are not only ignorant of time itself, but also of the motion of the celestial Bodies, which is the cause of it; we are only sensible of the different Effects it hath upon the Earth, as Heat, Cold, Wind, Rain, and such like various natural Productions; these things we are sooner and better acquainted with than you, so that you often predict
