Dialogue IV.

---

*Ulysses, Circe,* the *Goat.*

*Ulysses.* 'Twas always my opinion, most noble *Circe*, that there was a mighty difference between one Man and another, and we have a Proverb in *Greece* much to this purpose; but I could never have believed it had been so great, as now I am convinced it is since my last discourse with yonder *Hare*, or to express my self more properly, with him whom you transformed into that creature.

*Circe.* And why so? do you find him inclined to become Man again?

*Ulysses.*
