But Madam, to wave this discourse, I must intreat you to let me try my Luck once more, for I can hardly believe that all of them have so intirely lost the use of their Reason, as the three last seem to have done, whom, I must confess, you turned down to the lowest Class of Beasts not without a just cause, since as far as I can judge by their discourse, they were little better when they were Men.

*Circe.* I am content you shou'd try your Skill upon yonder *Hare*, whom you see feeding under the shade of that well-spread *Oak*. Go then and speak to him, for I have restored to him his Organs of Speech.

*Ulysses.* Don't run away, Friend, but stay here, and answer me a few questions, for *Circe* has assured me thou art able to do it.

*Hare.* Alas! what is the meaning of this? By what strange Fascination it has happen'd I can't tell, but I once more understand the signification of human discourse. Oh my untoward

destiny!
